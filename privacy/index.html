Privacy policy

Lock In for Android · Effective 5 November 2026

Lock In has no internet access. It can't send anything about you anywhere, and the developer never receives any of your data.

What Lock In collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or trackers. The app doesn't ask Android for internet access at all, so it has no way to connect to a server.

What stays on your phone

To work, Lock In saves a few settings on your phone:

  • the apps on your home screen and the apps you've chosen to block
  • your to-do tasks
  • your timer and pomodoro settings, and the session you're in
  • which answers you've given to its setup questions

Uninstalling Lock In, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

Permissions, and what each is used for

  • Installed apps: to show your apps on the home screen and in search.
  • Usage Access: while you're locked in, to see which app is on screen so a blocked app can be covered. This is checked as it happens and not saved.
  • Display over other apps: to show the lock-in screen over a blocked app.
  • Notification access (optional): during focus, to hide notifications from the apps you've blocked. Lock In looks only at which app a notification comes from. It doesn't read the title or text, and it saves nothing.
  • Notifications: to show that you're locked in.
  • Alarms & reminders, and vibration: to buzz on time when a focus block, a break or a session ends.
  • Unrestricted battery (optional): so your phone doesn't stop Lock In in the background, which would end blocking.
  • Wallpaper: to set a plain black wallpaper, only if you ask it to.
  • Uninstall apps: to open Android's uninstall prompt when you choose Uninstall on an app.

Backups

If you've turned on Google backup on your phone, Android may include Lock In's settings in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so they come back on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account. The developer can't see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Children

Lock In collects no data from anyone, including children.
